## Board Responsibilities

The MOSA Board of Directors is responsible for:

- Setting strategic direction for the organization
- Approving the annual budget and financial oversight
- Reviewing and approving fiscal sponsorship applications
- Ensuring compliance with nonprofit laws and regulations
- Representing MOSA to the broader community

## Join the Board

MOSA welcomes community members who are passionate about open source and want to help guide the organization. Board members serve volunteer terms and meet regularly (typically monthly).

If you're interested in board service, please [contact us](/contact/).
