## Board Meeting Schedule

MOSA board meetings are generally **open to the community**. All are welcome to attend and observe. Community members may speak during the public comment period.

:::note[Work in Progress]
The upcoming meeting schedule is being finalized. Check back soon or [contact us](/contact/) to be notified.
:::

## Meeting Format

Meetings are held **virtually via video call** on a regular cadence (typically monthly). Connection details are shared prior to each meeting.
