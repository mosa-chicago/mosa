## Typical Agenda Items

MOSA board meeting agendas generally include:

1. Call to order and roll call
2. Approval of previous meeting minutes
3. Financial report
4. Fiscal sponsorship updates
5. Program and initiative updates
6. New business
7. Public comment
8. Adjournment
