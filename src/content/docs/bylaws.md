## Overview

MOSA's bylaws govern the organization's structure, including:

- Membership structure and eligibility
- Board of Directors composition and election
- Officer roles and responsibilities
- Meeting procedures and voting requirements
- Financial controls and fiscal sponsorship policies
- Amendment procedures

## Nonprofit Status

Midwest Open Source Alliance is incorporated as an Illinois nonprofit corporation and holds **federal 501(c)(3) tax-exempt status** (EIN 83-1560285). Our bylaws comply with Illinois nonprofit corporation law and IRS requirements for public charities.

## Transparency

MOSA is committed to organizational transparency. Our financial information is available on [Candid/GuideStar](https://app.candid.org/profile/9737639/midwest-open-source-alliance-83-1560285/).
