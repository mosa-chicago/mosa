## Support Open Source Community

Your donation to Midwest Open Source Alliance helps fund:

- 🎪 **Community events** — keeping registration affordable and accessible
- 🎙️ **Live captioning** — making events accessible to the deaf and hard-of-hearing community
- 📹 **Session recordings** — preserving knowledge for those who can't attend in person
- 🌍 **Diversity & inclusion** — supporting underrepresented voices in open source
- 🏕️ **Camp scholarships** — helping community members attend events they otherwise couldn't

MOSA is a **501(c)(3) nonprofit** (EIN 83-1560285). All donations are tax-deductible to the extent permitted by law.
