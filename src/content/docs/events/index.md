MOSA serves as **fiscal sponsor** for open source events and communities. As a fiscal sponsor, we provide legal and financial infrastructure so organizers can focus on building community.

## Founding Sponsored Members

These communities helped establish MOSA as a fiscal sponsor:

- **[Midwest Area Drupal Camp (MidCamp)](https://www.midcamp.org/)** — founding member
- **[Twin Cities Drupal Camp](http://tcdrupal.org/)** — founding member
- **Midwest Drupal Summit** — founding member
