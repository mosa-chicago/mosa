## Drupal 25th Anniversary Gala

**March 24, 2026 · 7:00 PM – 10:00 PM CST**

[610 S Michigan Ave, Chicago, IL 60605](https://www.google.com/maps?q=41.8740396,-87.6247696)

---

For a quarter-century, Drupal has been more than just a software project—it has been a launchpad for careers, a place for lifelong friendships, and a powerful engine driving the Open Web.

To celebrate the 25th anniversary of Drupal during DrupalCon Chicago, the community came together for a night of connection, history, and fun.

### Highlights

- Curated culinary stations throughout the evening
- Dress code: "Drupal Fancy" — gowns, black tie, and Drupal tees equally welcome
- Music, dancing, and a night-long celebration of shared success
- A dedicated lower-volume sanctuary for deep conversation and community stories
- Inspiring milestone moments honoring the past, present, and future of Drupal

### Sponsorship

Instead of hosting a private event, companies were invited to participate in this shared community moment as a [sponsor](https://www.zeffy.com/en-US/ticketing/drupal-25th-anniversary-gala-sponsorship).

For more information, contact Gala Chair Tiffany Farriss (farriss@palantir.net).
