## Why Sponsor MOSA?

Sponsoring Midwest Open Source Alliance puts your organization at the heart of the Drupal and open source community in the Midwest and beyond. MOSA fiscally sponsors 13+ events and communities serving thousands of open source practitioners annually.

Your sponsorship supports:

- Community events, camps, and conferences
- Accessibility initiatives (live captioning, recordings)
- Diversity and inclusion programs
- Scholarships for community members to attend events
