## Fiscal Sponsorship

MOSA provides fiscal sponsorship for open source events and communities that align with our mission. As a fiscal sponsor, MOSA:

- Receives and disburses funds on behalf of the sponsored project
- Provides a 501(c)(3) umbrella for tax-deductible donations
- Handles tax filings and financial reporting for the project
- Provides organizational credibility to donors and sponsors

### Applying for Fiscal Sponsorship

To apply for fiscal sponsorship, [contact us](/contact/) with:

1. Description of your event or community
2. How it aligns with MOSA's mission (open source, Drupal community)
3. Expected annual budget
4. Geographic focus
5. Current organizational status

The MOSA board reviews applications at regular meetings.

## Event Support

MOSA can provide the following support to fiscally sponsored events:

- **Financial management** — receiving sponsorship payments and paying event expenses
- **Legal coverage** — operating under MOSA's nonprofit status
- **Insurance** — event liability coverage (where applicable)
- **Accounting** — annual financial reporting

Questions? [Contact us](/contact/).
