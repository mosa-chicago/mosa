## MOSA Bylaws

The bylaws of the Midwest Open Source Alliance govern how the organization operates. They define the structure of the board, the rights and responsibilities of members, meeting procedures, and other foundational rules.

> **Note:** The full text of the bylaws will be posted here. Please [contact us](/contact/) if you need a copy in the meantime.

## Summary of Key Provisions

### Organizational Purpose

MOSA is organized exclusively for educational and charitable purposes under Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code. Its purpose is to promote the education, support, and development of Drupal and related open source software.

### Board of Directors

- The organization is governed by a Board of Directors.
- Directors serve fixed terms as defined in the bylaws.
- The Board is responsible for setting policy and overseeing the organization's finances and operations.

### Amendments

The bylaws may be amended by a vote of the Board of Directors in accordance with the procedures set forth in the bylaws.
