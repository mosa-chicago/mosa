## Our Commitment

Midwest Open Source Alliance (MOSA) is committed to providing a welcoming, safe, and inclusive environment for everyone — regardless of gender, gender identity and expression, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, body size, race, age, religion, or national origin.

We expect all community members, speakers, sponsors, and staff to help us create a positive experience for everyone at MOSA-affiliated events, online spaces, and activities.

---

## Code of Conduct

MOSA adopts the **Drupal Community Code of Conduct** as the standard for our events and community interactions.

**[Read the full Drupal Community Code of Conduct →](https://www.drupal.org/dcoc)**

The Drupal Community Code of Conduct applies to all MOSA-affiliated:

- Events and camps we fiscally sponsor
- Online communication channels (GitHub, mailing lists, etc.)
- Board and committee meetings
- Any other activities carried out under the MOSA name

---

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, or have any other concerns, please report it as soon as possible by contacting the MOSA Board of Directors:

📧 **[info@midwestopensourcealliance.org](mailto:info@midwestopensourcealliance.org)**

All reports will be handled with discretion and confidentiality.

---

## Enforcement

MOSA reserves the right to take any action deemed appropriate in response to a code of conduct violation, up to and including removal from an event or community space and a ban from future participation.
