## Sponsored Events

MOSA provides fiscal sponsorship and support for open source events throughout the Midwest and beyond. As a 501(c)(3) organization, we enable events to accept tax-deductible donations and simplify financial operations so organizers can focus on building community.

- **[Live Captioning Initiative](https://github.com/MidCamp/live-captioning)** — Providing accessible live captions at events
