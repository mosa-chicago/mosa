## Sponsor MOSA

By sponsoring MOSA, you're investing in the open source community. Your support enables us to sustain our fiscal sponsorship program, support accessibility and inclusion initiatives, and help open source events thrive.

MOSA is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, so your sponsorship may be tax-deductible.

---

## Why Sponsor MOSA?

- **Reach the Drupal community:** MOSA supports events and initiatives attended by thousands of Drupal and open source developers, designers, and business leaders.
- **Demonstrate your commitment:** Show the open source community that your organization values community-driven development.
- **Tax benefit:** Sponsorships may be tax-deductible as a charitable contribution or business expense.
- **Make a real impact:** Your sponsorship directly enables community events and initiatives that might not otherwise be possible.
