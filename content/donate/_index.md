## What Your Donation Supports

MOSA uses donations to:

- Provide fiscal sponsorship services for open source events
- Support accessibility and inclusion initiatives at community events
- Fund the [Live Captioning Initiative](https://github.com/MidCamp/live-captioning)
- Support the [Drupal Recording Initiative](https://opencollective.com/drupal-recording-initiative)
- Help sustain the [Drupal Chicago Meetup group](https://www.meetup.com/drupalchicago/)
- Cover organizational operating costs so more community funds go directly to events
